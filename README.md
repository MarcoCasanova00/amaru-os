# AMARU OS / BUNKER ADVANCED

**AMARU OS** is an open-source custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1
synthesiser. It is a **four-track** generative engine for hard genres
(tekno, acid, hardcore, jungle, drum & bass, psytrance) — in continuous
evolution, with wavetable, FM, granular and analog synthesis all available
per track.

Tested on real FM-1 hardware. Built by musicians, for musicians — the
pursuance of trap groove, true dark atmosphere and in-pocket bounces.

---

## At a glance

- **4 tracks**: AIR (pad/chords), MELODY (lead), ROOT (bass), SPARK (drums)
- **9 first-class genres**: tribe tekno, mental tekno, hardtekk, acidcore,
  jungle, drum & bass, breakbeat tekno, techno, psytrance (DnB / breaks are not four-on-the-floor)
- **Deterministic generator**: GROW / EVOLVE / press OCT+ to generate a full take
  per seed
- **Three strict monochrome terminal themes**: green/black, black/white, amber/black
- **Native timbres**: Carnac- / Mycelium-style textures recreated by parameter
  value alone (no binaries imported)
- **License**: GPL-3.0-only (derivative of Bunker Evolved)

## Try it now — no download

Open https://marcocasanova00.github.io/amaru-os/ → click `Einschalten` → connect
the FM-1 by USB for the live version.

## What you'll hear

Two-voice synthesis per track, sequenced in a GEN page. `GENRE / SEED / ENERGY / FORM`
on K1–K4. The output targets dark techno and acidcore energy, while the
 generators still keep the swing and rhythmic cornerstones of breaks, jungle,
drum & bass, and hardtek.

## Tips

- **OCT+** for a new take from the same parameters.
- **OCT-** resets the seed.
- **Sound display** shows the recipe source (AMARU ECHO, MAYA PLUCK,
  AMARU BASS, GREEN KIT, …) as playbacks unfold.
- See `docs/QUICKSTART.md` for commands, themes, and GEN navigation.
